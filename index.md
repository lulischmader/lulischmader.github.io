**Welcome**

I am an Ecuadorian scholar working at the intersections of Digital Humanities, Indigenous feminisms, Latin American Studies, and environmental humanities.

My research examines Indigenous women’s resistance to extractivist development in the Ecuadorian Amazon through ethnography, oral history, documentary practice, and digital storytelling. I focus on Indigenous sovereignty, environmental justice, and decolonial feminist praxis.

This website features my research, documentary work, teaching, and digital humanities projects.
